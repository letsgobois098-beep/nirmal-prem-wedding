OUR HERITAGE

The Nirmal Prem Story

Founded in 1948 shortly after Indian independence at the historic Gandhi Gate in Tohana, Nirmal Prem began with a steadfast commitment: providing pure textiles, honest pricing, and unmatched personal hospitality to families in Haryana and neighboring Punjab.

Over the decades, as wedding ceremonies transformed into grand cinematic celebrations, Nirmal Prem evolved gracefully alongside our clients. What began as a trusted traditional fabric destination has blossomed into an all-encompassing bridal and festive fashion landmark housing three distinct divisions: Wedding Plaza, DressUp, and the Rental Studio.

Grandmothers. Mothers. Daughters.

Three generations of brides have walked through our doors at Gandhi Gate, Tohana, trusting us to weave the magic for the most unforgettable morning of their lives.
